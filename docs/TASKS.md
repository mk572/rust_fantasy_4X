**438. Battle AI: kiting and not stepping into a worse fight (follow-up to 232).** In `battle/ai.rs` a shooter holds and fires but does not back away from a melee unit that can reach it, and a unit steps next to an enemy stack without weighing what the stack and its neighbours do to it in the reply. (1) Kiting: a shooter with moves left and a faster or equal-speed melee enemy in reach retreats out of its reach before it shoots, when it can still shoot from there. (2) Before a step, compare the expected damage taken from every enemy that can reach the square with the damage dealt from it (`expected_damage`, `worth`), and prefer a square that gives a better exchange or wait. Sections 6.3 and 6.4. Measure with `cargo test -p f4x-learn --test it mirror -- --ignored`; the learned player relies on `ai::next_command` giving unit orders only. Sallying out is in task 370.
