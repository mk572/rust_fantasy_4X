# Lairs (guarded sites)

Source: ReMoM `Create_Lair`, `Generate_Lairs`, `Lair_Make_Guardians`, `Lair.c`. Code: `crates/f4x-core/src/feature/lair.rs`; the numbers are data: `content/lairs.ron` (struct `LairRules`, `crates/f4x-core/src/rules/lair.rs`, whose defaults are the original's; a test keeps the file equal to them, a mod may change the file or leave it out).

- Every tower, node and encounter site gets a `Lair` (guards + hoard) at map generation; towers share one on both planes.
- Guards are neutral units made on entry (`Command::EnterSite`), fought with the battle model, then removed. Winning takes the hoard and clears the site (encounter sites vanish; towers and nodes stay).
- Moving onto a guarded site gives `Event::SiteEncounter` and nothing moves; an unguarded site pays out on arrival.

Differences from the original (gap tasks in TASKS.md):
- Strong/weak split of encounter sites is random (25:32), not fixed counts; site types follow the data, not a random 7-way roll.
- The lair-race switch fall-through (ReMoM-marked bug) is fixed: death 2, chaos 2, nature 1 in five.
- Spell rewards (task 305): a spell of a realm the wizard has books in, or arcane, with the original's rarity caps; see `divergences.md` section 10. Book and retort rewards follow the intent, not the original's muddled index arithmetic.
- Items are real: each hoard value is made into an item (`make_item`, `Grade::Any`) and goes to the armory (vault first), and the client opens the item screen after the treasure box. A prisoner is a hero offered for nothing (`Offer::Hero` with price 0, first on the offers; hired at the summoning circle, where the original's bug moves it to the lair). A book or retort hoard that gives nothing pays an item of 1200 or 1000 mana instead.
- The client shows the original's boxes (`site_prompt.rs`): the Yes/No question with the site's picture, and the treasure box (`--site ask|found|treasure` for screenshots). Missing: the discovery music and the colour slide of the node pictures.
- Checked against `Create_Lair` and `Lair_Make_Guardians` (17 October 2026): the guard choice (dearest unit under a share of the budget, first of equals, up to 8 of the first kind, the second kind from what is left, nine in all), the hoard rolls and the treasure box texts follow the original; the differences are the ones above. The battlefield of a site has its building in the middle (`Structure`), which nobody can enter.
- A winner takes only what it can use (task 353, `claim_lair`): what it cannot (a spell, book or retort for a neutral city's player, items for a player with no hero, a freed prisoner when the hero roster is full or the neutral city has no room) is rerolled from its value with the hoard's table (`reroll_hoard`), as gold, mana and (with a hero) items. A prisoner joins a neutral winner as a hero in its nearest city with room; a wizard still gets the free offer.
- Neutral cities raid (task 354, `f4x_ai::neutral`): a city with a site it has explored within 8 squares builds a party of 3 beyond its garrison, sends it to a free square next to the site, assaults it with the battle AI playing both sides, and goes home. Caveat: a city sees only about 2 squares, so raids are rare until neutrals scout.
- Who knows the guards: a player knows what guards a site once they have fought for it (win, loss or flight; `Lair::fought_by`, saved with the site), or while a spell shows them the units there: Nature Awareness (the whole map) and Nature's Eye (the squares of its city's sight). Awareness shows only cities and True Sight only unmasks illusions, so neither counts. The map shows the guards for such a site: the hover text lists them (`ui.lair_guard`) and the surveyor names them, else it says "Guards unknown" (`surveyor.guards_unknown`). The question box on entering still names the first guard kind, as the original does. The neutral cities' raids skip a known site whose guards are too strong for the party (`PlayerView::lair_guards`).
- The rules as data (task 355, checked against `Generate_Lairs` and `Create_Lair` on 23 October 2026): the tower budget (650 + 50 x Random(11)), the node budget (power squared x (4 + Random(11)) x (magic + 1) / 2), the strong and weak encounter budgets for each plane, the realm splits, the share of the budget for each guard kind, the cap of 9 guards, the plane multipliers of the hoard and the 15-slot hoard table are in `lairs.ron`. A test (`generated_lairs_for_a_seed_stay_the_same`) holds a checksum of the lairs of five seeds, which the move did not change. Differences kept: the node budget is capped at 32767 where the original's 16-bit value would wrap; the strong/weak counts are `GenParams` (25 and 32), placed by the map generator and not by `Generate_Lairs`; the reroll table of task 353 (`reroll_hoard`) keeps its own numbers.
- The wizards' AI raids sites (task 356, `f4x_ai::site`): after settling and scouting, one party at a time of three or more soldiers beyond a city's garrison leaves for an explored, intact site within 10 squares, if it is 1.4 times as strong as the guards: the guards it knows (`PlayerView::lair_guards`) or, for an unknown site, a guess of 2.5 average soldiers of its army. It walks to a free square next to the site, assaults with `Command::EnterSite` (the battle AI fights), strikes the next site in range and comes home when none is left. The army (`military.rs`) and the scouts leave the raiders alone while they raid, and no party leaves while the army marches. Loot is used by what the AI had: items are equipped, the free prisoner is hired, gold and mana go to the plans. Not done: melding a cleared node with a spirit (the magic AI). The learned battle player (`f4x-learn`) is a battle brain and plays these battles like any other; its mirror test (`-- --ignored`) was not run for this task.
