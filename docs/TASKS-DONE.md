## Descriptions

**62. Player-chosen losses when mana runs short (done).** Not in the original (its `WIZ_MatchManaUpkeep` drops a random kind of asset and only the summons part exists), so this is our own rule, chosen by the project owner: before ending the turn the client asks `Game::upkeep_shortfall` and, if the reserve plus the coming mana income cannot pay, shows a prompt (`upkeep_prompt.rs`) with every item that costs mana, the default pre-marked; `Command::ReleaseUpkeep` gives up the chosen ones and the turn change drops only what is still missing. The AI and the console keep the deterministic newest-first rule. The "mana short" event interaction belongs to task 68 (random events).

**68. Random events (done).** Plague, population boom, conjunctions, moons, mana short and the other eighteen events of the original, modelled in `docs/events.md` (the table, the draw and its vetoes, the victim weights, how each ends, what each does); switchable option [768-775, 683]. Follow intent for B18 (count from the real turn, not from game start or load). Events are a table of statuses, not enchantments with timers. Done: `events.rs` (the table, the pressure, the victim weights, the end rules), `events_game.rs` (the draw with its vetoes and the playing out of all 18 events), the readers (mana short and conjunctions in the power, the moons in the temples' mana, the boom in growth), the saved state, the pop-up (`event_popup.rs`, from `EVENTMSG.LBX` and `EVENTS.LBX`), the world event under the minimap, the plague and boom marks in the city header, `--event KIND` for screenshots and the console's `event`. What is left is task 303.

**72. Fame effects (done).** Fame sources (heroes, Just Cause, Famous retort) and effects on unit upkeep
and hero availability [765]; follow the help for legendary heroes (+3 fame per level, B12). Design: [`heroes-items-design.md`](heroes-items-design.md) section 3.7: `fame::total` (earned fame, Legendary heroes, Just Cause) replaces the plain `Player.fame` in `finance.rs` and the offers. **Done:** the sources and effects were already built with tasks 47, 62 and 220 and the conquest and battle work (`Game::fame`, `finance.rs`, `offers.rs`); this task checked them against the help and corrected topic 765 (`divergences.md` section 23).

**74. Stable command references (done).** Commands carry `hecs` entity ids, which change when a game is loaded or an undo rebuilds the world, so a command log could not cross a load. **Done for local play** (replays and saving a game with its history): `replay.rs` writes every accepted command with creation numbers (`Serial`) in place of its entities (`serde(with)` helpers on the `Command` fields, `Target` and `ItemSpot`), keeps the start snapshot beside them (`Game::start_recording`, `stop_recording`, `replay_so_far`), and `Replay::play` rebuilds the game from the snapshot and applies the commands, resolving the numbers against the game it is playing into; a refused or undone command is dropped from the record, and a replayed game goes on recording. Console: `record [start|stop [name]|status]`, `replay [name] [count]` (`saves/<name>.replay.ron`). `Command` itself is unchanged, so no call site moved. Not done: the client has no replay menu, and a battle in progress cannot be recorded (a snapshot holds none). Multiplayer is not part of it: `PLANNED.md` item 36 (to be discussed).
