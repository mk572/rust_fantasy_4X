**654. (done) Remove trivial wrappers.** Asked for on 17 November 2026. Two kinds, handled differently. **708. Damage type cleanup (done).** Damage and immunities do not work in an intuitive way. Today an attack has one nature (`AttackNature` in `battle/immunity.rs`: a normal weapon, a missile, fire, a magical shot, an illusion) and an immunity of the target stops the attack as a whole. Flame Blade, Holy Weapon and Eldritch Weapon give the `magical_weapon` ability (41), which turns the whole blow magical: it ignores weapon immunity, but the fire it is named for is nowhere, so fire immunity does nothing against it. The types are spread over three things that do not agree: the nine immunities (`Immunities::of`, hard-coded ability numbers), the realm of an attack, and `DamageKind` in `spells.ron` (set on every damage spell, read by no rule). The same nature is hard-coded in 8 places (`vortex.rs`, `battle/mod.rs` Wall of Fire, `attack.rs` Immolation, `field.rs`, `spell/city.rs`, `spell/world.rs`, `spell.rs`, `casting.rs`).
Gaps found in the audit: the unit Fireball (22, about 20 attackers) is stopped by magic immunity only, not fire immunity; the unit Ice Bolt (29: wind mage, djinn) not by cold immunity; the item powers with an `attack:` field (Lightning 1, Chaos 101, Destruction 100, Death 99, Vampiric 35, Stoning 66, Phantasmal 30) have no type in the data. To check against the original before changing: Psionic Blast has `immune: [31, 40]` (illusion immunity is no damage type); the unit Death Bolt (14) and Drow Bolt (18) count as chaos realm; lightning has no immunity at all (fine if meant).
TO BE:
1. **One list of damage types, in the data.** It holds the elements (fire, cold, lightning, poison, stoning, death), the forms (mundane weapon, missile, magic), the realms (nature, sorcery, chaos, life, death, arcane) and the delivery (melee, ranged). `DamageKind`, `AttackNature.realm` and `AttackNature.stopped_by` are replaced by it.
2. **An attack carries a set of types**, so it can be fire and chaos, or fire and nature: Fire Bolt is {ranged, fire, chaos, magic}, a Ranger's arrow {ranged, missile, weapon}, the unit Fireball {ranged, fire, chaos, magic}, the unit Ice Bolt {ranged, cold, nature, magic}.
3. **Damage is kept in parts, one per source** (the unit's own attack, an item, an enchantment, an item power), each part with its own type set. A spearman with Flame Blade deals a weapon part (its base attack) and a fire part (the enchantment). A target with weapon immunity ignores the weapon part and takes the fire part; a target with fire immunity takes the weapon part and ignores the fire part. Only the parts the target is immune to are lost.
