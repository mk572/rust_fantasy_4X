Sheets

A sheet of the source folder is cut into one PNG per picture in the target folder.

On the combat grid

A square is the 32 x 16 diamond of the combat grid; the picture stands on the square's bottom, centred across it (the original's city walls, `citywall_NNN_FF.png`, are where the game draws them). The red guide on the canvas is the square at that place. Crop bottom clears what hangs over the square's lower edges.

Palette (256 colours)

Index 0 is transparent. Right-click a pixel to pick its colour. Indexes 224 to 255 are black in the base palette: the original's screens fill them.
