Fantasy 4X for macOS (Apple silicon)
====================================

1. Drag Fantasy4X.app to your Applications folder.

2. The app is not signed by Apple (the author has no developer account), so macOS blocks
   the first start. Open it once with a right click (or Control-click) on the app, choose
   Open, then Open again in the dialog. If macOS says the app is damaged, open Terminal
   and run:

       xattr -cr /Applications/Fantasy4X.app

3. This download contains none of the original Master of Magic's pictures, sounds or music.
   The game starts without them, in plain colours and silent. To get the pictures and sound,
   run the Fantasy 4X setup program on your own copy of the original game.

Your saves and the pictures and sounds the setup makes are in
~/Library/Application Support/Fantasy4X/ . Delete that folder to remove them.
